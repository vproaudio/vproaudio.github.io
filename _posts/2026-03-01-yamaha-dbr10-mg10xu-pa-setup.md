Basic PA System Setup (2 × Yamaha DBR10 + Yamaha MG10XU)

## ⚙️ Equipment

- 1 × Yamaha MG10XU mixer
- 2 × Yamaha DBR10 powered speakers
- 2 × XLR cables



## 🔌 Before You Begin

- Keep the mixer and both speakers powered **OFF** before starting
- Set the mixer's **STEREO OUT** fader to the lowest position (0)
- Set the **CH 2 GAIN** knob on each DBR10 speaker to **-∞** (fully counter-clockwise)



## 🔗 Step 1: Connect the Cables

1. Run an XLR cable from the mixer's **STEREO OUT** (main output) to **Channel 2** on the first DBR10 speaker
2. Run a second XLR cable from the mixer's **STEREO OUT** to **Channel 2** on the second DBR10 speaker



## 🟢 Step 2: Power On Sequence

1. Switch on the **mixer** first, with **STEREO OUT** still set to 0
2. Switch on **each DBR10 speaker**
3. On each speaker, slowly bring the **Channel 2 gain** up to **0 dB**
4. Finally, bring the mixer's **STEREO OUT** fader up to **0 dB**

> ⚠️ Always power on the mixer before the speakers, and always raise the mixer's output last. This avoids sending a power-on pop or surge through the speakers.



## 🔴 Step 3: Power Off / Disconnect Sequence

1. Lower the mixer's **STEREO OUT** fader to its **lowest position (0)**
2. On each DBR10 speaker, turn the **Channel 2 gain** down to **-∞**
3. Switch **off** each DBR10 speaker
4. Switch **off** the mixer last



## 🔁 Final Check

- ✅ STEREO OUT lowered before touching any speaker controls
- ✅ Both speaker channel 2 gains at -∞ before power off
- ✅ Speakers powered off before the mixer
- ✅ Mixer is the first device on and the last device off



## 💡 Pro Tips

- Always power gear on in this order: mixer → speakers → raise levels
- Always power gear off in reverse: lower levels → speakers → mixer
- Bringing levels up/down gradually protects your speakers from pops and sudden volume spikes
