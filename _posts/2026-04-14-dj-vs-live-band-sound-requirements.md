Choosing between a DJ and a live band changes more than the vibe of your reception — it changes the sound system you need behind the scenes.

## What a DJ Typically Needs

- A mixer or controller with at least 2 line inputs
- 2 main speakers, positioned for even dance floor coverage
- A subwoofer for larger rooms or bass-heavy genres
- A wireless mic for announcements and toasts

## What a Live Band Typically Needs

- A larger format mixer with enough channels for vocals, drums, and instruments
- Monitor speakers (wedges or in-ears) so the band can hear themselves
- More microphones — vocal, instrument, and drum overheads
- More power and more cable runs, so plan for extra load-in time

## Questions to Ask Your Rental Company

- Does the package include monitors, or just front-of-house speakers?
- How many mic inputs does the mixer support?
- Who runs sound during the reception — you, the band, or an on-site tech?

## Bottom Line

A DJ setup can usually run on a lean 2-speaker system, while a live band needs a more complete stage rig with monitors and extra mic channels. Tell us which one you're booking and we'll recommend the right package.
